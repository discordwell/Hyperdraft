## Zones

Deck, hand, three battlefield columns (the 3×3 craft grid), biome row (mana sources you mined this game), End raid track, discard. There's no graveyard graveyard — sunk cards go to discard and reshuffle when the deck empties.

## Turn structure

Day/Night flip · Draw 1 · Start-of-turn bonuses (biome income, structure passives) · Main phase (mine biomes for resources, deploy creatures into the 3 columns, attack column-to-column, craft from your hand). No priority stack, no combat sub-phase — actions resolve in the order you fire them.

## Win condition

Raid the End and defeat the Ender Dragon, or reduce your opponent's avatar to 0 HP. Day/night flips passively change which cards are stronger — Skeletons and Endermen flourish at night.

## One quirk to know

**The board is three columns, not one big front line.** Creatures attack the column directly across from them; if a column is empty, damage goes face. You can't just pile every Worker on the left — opening a flank lets unblocked damage through, which is how raid decks actually finish.
