## Zones

Hand (5 cards, refills when both players empty), deck, discard, three scoring piles per player — **Territory** (cap 8), **Nap** (cap 6), **Snack** (cap 5) — an unlimited **Attention** pile for tiebreakers and overflow, a one-slot **Command** seat holding your pre-game Commander Cat, and Claw (exile).

## Turn structure

A *round*, not a turn — both players act each round. Stretch (round-start triggers, untap pile cards) · Pounce (the follower commits one card face-up) · Counter-Pounce (the lead reacts with one card) · Resolve trick (compare under the category's rule) · Claim (winner picks which pile takes both cards) · Curl Up (end triggers, hand refresh check, round counter increments).

## Win condition

Nine rounds, then score: Territory 1/card with bonuses, Nap 2/card capped at 12, Snack 3/card but only if the pile has under 5. Highest total wins; ties broken by Attention pile size; further ties are draws.

## One quirk to know

**The pile a card lives in is its ability, not its name.** A Cat in your Nap pile activates the Nap ability; the same card in your Snack pile activates the Snack one. You won the trick — now decide what this card *is*. That single Claim choice carries the entire strategy.
