## Zones

Deck, hand (cap 8), the battlefield (all Vessels, Mines, attached Crew/Weapons live here), Wreckage (graveyard), Lost at Sea (exile). Every Vessel also carries a **depth band** — Surface, Periscope, Mid, Deep, Crush — which is public state, not a zone.

## Turn structure

Dive (untap, gain 1 Torpedo + 1 Sonar Charge up to the per-turn cap, draw 1) · Maneuver (deploy, attach, change depth, lay mines, play Doctrines) · Engagement (declare attackers, **Detection resolution** — defender spends Sonar to ping incoming subs, declare interceptors only against detected attackers, simultaneous damage with depth modifiers) · Regroup (post-engagement main) · Surface (discard to 8, detection pings expire, oxygen ticks down on submerged Vessels).

## Win condition

Sink the opponent's Flagship (25 hull, locked at Periscope) or wipe their fleet — no Vessels on the board *and* none in hand or deck.

## One quirk to know

**An undetected Vessel can't be intercepted.** That's the whole game. You spend Sonar Charges to detect; the opponent spends Sonar to stay quiet. A Silent Hunter deck can swing through every turn untargetable until the defender finds the Sonar to ping it — and a depth charge that fires at Mid can't reach Crush at all, so picking the right depth is half of every attack.
