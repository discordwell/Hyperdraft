## Zones

Deck, hand, Active spot (the one Pokémon currently fighting), Bench (up to 5), Prize cards (6, set aside face-down at game start), discard pile, Stadium slot, Lost Zone.

## Turn structure

Draw 1 · Main phase (play Basics, evolve, attach one Energy, play Trainers, retreat, use abilities — in any order) · Attack (your Active uses one attack, ending the turn) · Checkup (between turns: Poison, Burn, Sleep, Paralysis tick down). Per-turn caps: 1 Energy attachment, 1 Supporter, 1 Stadium, 1 retreat.

## Win condition

Take all 6 of your Prize cards. You take one each time you knock out an opposing Pokémon — so KOs *are* the score. Decking out and having an empty Bench when your Active is KO'd both also lose.

## One quirk to know

**Prizes are set aside before the game starts, drawn from your own deck.** That means the six best cards in your list might be locked in the prize pile, unreachable until you start scoring KOs. Building around this — running multiple copies of your key pieces — is the entire deckbuilding problem.
