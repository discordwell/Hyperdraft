## Zones

Deck, hand (10 max), board (7 minion slots per side), graveyard, secrets area, hero, hero power. Boards are the whole arena — no separate combat row.

## Turn structure

Draw step (gain a mana crystal, draw one, fatigue if the deck is empty) · Main phase (play cards, attack, fire hero power, all in any order) · End step. There's no priority stack and no combat phase — when you attack, you point and click.

## Win condition

Kill the opposing hero (30 HP, sometimes armored). Fatigue damage from drawing on an empty deck scales by 1 each draw, so a long game ends itself.

## One quirk to know

**Mana is a ramp on rails.** You start with 1 crystal on turn 1, gain one per turn, cap at 10. There's no land flooding, no mana screw, no choice about your curve — every game is the same economic shape, which is why deck *tempo* matters more than ramp. Battlecries and deathrattles fire on enter and on death respectively; taunt forces attacks to go through it first.
