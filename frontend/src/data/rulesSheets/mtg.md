## Zones

Library, hand, battlefield, graveyard, exile, stack, command. The stack is the workspace where spells and abilities wait to resolve — anything cast or triggered queues there before it touches the board.

## Turn structure

Beginning (untap, upkeep, draw) · Pre-combat main · Combat (begin, attackers, blockers, damage, end) · Post-combat main · Ending (end step, cleanup). Players pass priority back and forth inside each step; nothing happens while someone still has something to say.

## Win condition

Reduce the opponent to 0 life, or force a draw they can't make. Twelve real Standard sets are loaded — Eldraine through Final Fantasy — so the legal pool is the actual current game.

## One quirk to know

**The stack resolves last-in-first-out.** Cast Lightning Bolt at a creature, opponent responds with Counterspell, you respond with Negate — Negate resolves first, then Counterspell fizzles, then your Bolt finally lands. The fights happen on top of the spell, not in place of it.
