## Zones

The Book (deck), Hand (max 7), Trading Floor (battlefield — Traders, Assets, Structures), Liquidated (graveyard), Written Off (exile), the Dark Pool (shared face-down slot for one ambush Order), the Derivatives Desk (per-player staging for unattached Derivative cards).

## Turn structure

Pre-Market (untap, collect passive income, pay interest, refill Liquidity) · Research (draw 1) · Trading Session (play cards, declare attackers, declare blockers, simultaneous damage) · Settlement (post-combat main) · Market Close (end-of-turn triggers, discard to 7).

## Win condition

Reduce the opponent's Capital Reserve from 30 to 0. One mythic, "Monopoly Position," wins instead at 20 Portfolio Value counters — a slow combo route, not a default plan.

## One quirk to know

**Leverage counters cost you life at end of turn.** A Trader with two Leverage counters might swing for +4/+0, but every Market Close it bills 1 Capital Reserve until you can pay it off or remove the counters. Leverage is real damage — borrowed power that the market reclaims if you can't close out.
