## Zones

Main Deck (40+), Extra Deck (Fusion / Synchro / XYZ monsters, summoned from here), hand, 5 Monster Zones, 5 Spell/Trap Zones, Field Spell slot, Graveyard, Banished pile.

## Turn structure

Draw · Standby · Main Phase 1 (summon, set, activate) · Battle Phase (attackers only — skipped on turn 1) · Main Phase 2 · End (discard to 6). One Normal Summon per turn, period.

## Win condition

Reduce opponent's Life Points from 8000 to 0. Decking out also loses — and there's no fatigue cushion, the draw itself is the lose condition. A handful of cards have alternate-win text on them ("Exodia," etc.) and those resolve as printed.

## One quirk to know

**Chains resolve last-in-first-out.** Spell A activates, opponent chains Trap B on top, you chain Counter Trap C on top of B — C resolves first, then B (if still legal), then A. Counter Traps (Spell Speed 3) can only be answered by other Counter Traps. The chain is why face-down Set spells and traps are load-bearing: they're the only way to interrupt the opponent's turn.
