## Zones

Hand (refills to 7 if you accept the Allocate-phase query), library, scrap heap (your "discard" — destroyed parts and spent transients), assembly floor (chassis + solo parts on the board), up to three structures (passive workshop fixtures), and a single command seat holding your pre-game **Core** — the AI personality that buffs your archetype.

## Turn structure

Six phases per turn: **Boot** (untap exhausted parts, trigger upkeep), **Allocate** (top off Compute; optional refill to 7 cards), **Assemble** (play chassis + parts, attach, activate abilities, all using Compute), **Combat** (declare attacking chassis, defender assigns blockers), **Reassemble** (post-combat repairs, burn leftover Compute for scrap), **Cleanup** (end-of-turn triggers, damage on chassis persists).

## Win condition

Reduce the opponent's **Workshop Integrity** from 25 to 0. Damage you deal that isn't blocked by chassis lands on the workshop. The longer the game runs the more dangerous things get: once the **Deathclock** activates, each round of unblocked damage doubles.

## One quirk to know

**Parts don't exist without a chassis.** A weapon or add-on played without a host runs at 1/1 — fragile, easy to pick off. Build chassis first, slot parts into them. The **Compute** economy means you usually can't both summon a frame and arm it the same turn; the workshop is always one tick behind the plan. Cores tilt this rhythm: FORGE-Δ discounts weapons, ETHOS-7 draws extra, MIRTHBOT-1 likes swarms, BULWARK-9 protects integrity.
