# Finance TCG — Strategy Doc

Living document of accumulated wisdom from LLM-pilot ultra-loop iterations.

## Format basics

- Win condition: opponent's Capital Reserve (life) reaches 0; both players start at 30.
- Resource: **Liquidity** (mana). Increments by 1 per turn from 0, capped at 10. Refills each turn.
- Hand limit: 7 at end of turn.
- Phase order per turn: PRE_MARKET → RESEARCH → TRADING_SESSION → SETTLEMENT → MARKET_CLOSE. Pilots act during TRADING_SESSION; combat resolves in SETTLEMENT.
- Card types: **TRADER** (creature), **ORDER** (instant), **STRATEGY** (sorcery), **ASSET** (enchantment), **STRUCTURE** (cap 3 per side), **DERIVATIVE** (auras/equipment; auto-attach to highest-power Trader).

## Engine quirks

- DERIVATIVES auto-attach to your highest-power Trader on play; if none, they stage in the Derivatives Desk.
- Alpha Strike (key keyword): when this attacks alone, opponent can't play Orders until Market Close (next turn's reset). Disables instant-speed responses.
- Arbitrage N (Risk Manager etc.): when this blocks, remove N damage from it after combat (effectively healing-on-block).
- Summoning sickness: Traders can't attack the turn they enter.
- **Combat overflow is trample-style by default** (iter-1, both pilots): excess Aggression beyond a blocker's remaining Defense Rating spills to the defender's Capital Reserve. There is no explicit Trample keyword — every Trader has it implicitly. Pilot A observed: 6-power attacker into 4-tough blocker = blocker dies + 2 face. Pilot B observed: 4/1 RFC into 1/2 Clerk = Clerk dies + 2 face. **Plan all blocks assuming overflow.** Even chumps leak 2-3 face.
- **Damage persists between combats within a turn?** Open question — unverified. Both pilots reported "any damage = death" for blocked Traders, but the trample-overflow numbers suggest the damage threshold is the printed Defense Rating, not 1. The effective rule: blocker dies if `attacker_aggression >= blocker_remaining_defense`, and `(attacker_aggression - blocker_remaining_defense)` overflows to face.

## Iteration 3 lessons (Quant vs Dark Arbitrage)

Block Trade Sweep, Hidden Aggression, Off-Exchange Position, Iceberg Order, Crossed Market — all 6 Dark Pool Orders are zero-effect. Their `dark_effect_fn` (wrapped in `dark_pool_setup`) never registers because Orders never enter battlefield (where `setup_interceptors` runs). Hidden Accumulator's "+1/+1 per DP cast" never fires; HA is a vanilla 2/2.
- **Dark Arbitrage's REAL identity (revealed by iter-3): wide-board midrange goodstuff Trader chain.** With 16+ cheap Trader bodies (4× DIP 2/3 ETB-tutor, 4× HACC 2/2, 2× OEO 3/3, 2× IBT 3/4 + ramp ETB, 4× OEF 3/4 + Alpha+4), the deck overwhelms Quant on board count once PCD dies. Plays 2 Traders/turn from T10 onwards. Dark Pool flavor is decorative.

- **Institutional Block Trader (5c, 3/4 Lev 2 Arb 1 +2 mana ETB) is a major tempo card.** Untested in iter-1/iter-2; iter-3 showed it lets P2 chain another play same turn. Carry forward.
- **Killing PCD remains mandatory for either side.** Quant lost PCD on T11 (blocked once = dead from any-damage-kills bug). Without PCD's +0/+1 lord, every Quant body collapsed from 4-tough to 3-tough. **PCD is irreplaceable in the current Quant pool — no PCD2.**

- **Damage-vs-toughness model is INCONSISTENT.** Pilot B observed BOTH behaviours in the same game: T11 SPB tough 4 took 3 dmg → DIED (any-dmg-kills); T13 OEO tough 3 took 2 dmg → SURVIVED (proper rules). - **Leverage tick math reconciled correctly this game.** Pilot A observed 2 self-damage from OEF Lev 2 at MC, then 4 from OEF+ICBT next MC. No doubling. Iter-2 bug 10/14 (×1.7 multiplier, ETB double-add) may be triggered only by ETB path — not observed in iter-3. The `leverage_bug_multiplier = 1.7` safety factor in `_filter_trap_cards` may be too conservative; consider re-tuning to 1.0 once a unit test confirms ETB path is the only doubler.
- **Rebalancing Halt is sorcery-speed in practice.** Cast at instant speed during opp's block-window: TAP fires but the targeted attacker stays in the declared-attackers list and damage resolves. Effective only when cast pre-declare on YOUR turn. NEW bug 17.
- **Off-Exchange Finisher's Alpha+4 fires in multi-attack.** Confirmed iter-1 bug 2 also affects OEF's `_make_alpha_strike_plus4(obj)` helper, not just standard alpha. 7 power with 4 attackers declared (should be 3 if "alone" rule applied). **AI heuristic implication**: P2 should always declare OEF FIRST in multi-attack to preserve the +4 buff (the asymmetry HELPS the attacker). ## Iteration 2 lessons (Derivatives vs Dark Arbitrage)

Two LLM pilots played the FINA_derivatives (P1) vs FINA_dark_arbitrage (P2) matchup. **P2 won at T9** but the result is uninformative for archetype balance — P1 self-destructed in MARKET_CLOSE on T9 from accumulated Leverage tick damage. P2 dealt zero combat damage and made only 3 plays. Sparse strategic signal; both pilots agree the game was decided by P1's self-damage, not any interaction.

- **Leverage Tick is the silent killer of Derivatives.** Every Leverage Trader deals N self-damage at the controller's MARKET_CLOSE per Leverage counter on it. Stacking 4-5 Leverage Traders without counter-removal cards on board (Theta Decay Trader pre-MC ability, Black-Scholes pay-1 trigger, Theta Decay Collar) is a guaranteed loss in 2-3 turns. P1 went 12 → -4 in a single MARKET_CLOSE phase on T9 with 5 Leverage Traders deployed.
- **Derivatives deck identity revised.** It is **NOT a "stack Derivatives auras on one Trader"** archetype — both pilots converged on this. It is a **"Leverage-counter-management"** archetype: Leverage = double-edged (+1/+0 power per counter, -1 capital per counter at MC). The win condition is *managing* the counter tax via Theta Decay Trader, Black-Scholes Model, and Theta Decay Collar — not piling on Derivative auras. See `docs/decks/FINA_derivatives_plan.md`.
- **Dark Arbitrage's combo identity is UNVERIFIED.** P2 deployed exactly 1 Trader and 1 Order across 9 turns. Hidden Accumulator, Hidden Aggression, and Block Trade Sweep — the deck's combo finishers — were all bricked. P2 reported "Dark Arbitrage is structurally dead on T1-T2" (no 1-cost Trader, no useful 1-cost Order without a DP follow-up). Tag for iter-3: needs a clean game where the combo actually fires. See `docs/decks/FINA_dark_arbitrage_plan.md`.
- **Suspected leverage-tick math bug.** Pilot A observed 16 self-damage in a single MC where the sum of Leverage counters across own Traders was 9. Pilot B's counts also failed to reconcile (predicted 8, observed 16). Hypothesis: tick fires twice per MC, OR counter add is doubling on ETB (event + direct setter both stacking), OR LIFE_CHANGE is being applied to wrong player on top of the legitimate tick. Until fixed, treat leverage tick as `Σleverage × ~1.7` for capital projections.

## Iteration 1 lessons (HF vs Quant, both pilots agree)

Both pilots played the same 28-turn game from opposite sides and converged on the same key takeaways:

- **HF vs Quant is roughly 45/55 in Quant's favor with blocks fixed.** HF lands 8-12 chip damage in T1-T9 from Alpha Strike pressure, but Quant's 4-tough Arbitrage walls (Risk Manager 1/4, Pairs Trader 2/3, Correlation Trader 2/4, Portfolio Construction Desk 3/4 lord) build faster than HF can break through with 1-2 toughness Traders. Quant wins by trader-count value engine + multi-attacker swarm closing once the wall is built.
- **Quant's win condition is board count, not big single threats.** Once Quant has 3+ Traders vs HF's 0-1, every Arbitrage trigger fires, Liquidity refills, and HF runs out of cards trying to keep tempo. HF's hand was empty by T20.
- **Alpha Strike "solo" math is fragile in the long game.** A solo 5-power swing into a 4-tough Arbitrage 1 blocker = trade of attacker for blocker + 1-2 overflow. Not enough damage per card spent once Quant stabilises.
- **Multi-attack is strictly worse than solo when Alpha is +3.** Pilot A confirmed: when 2 attackers are declared, only the first declared gets the +3 alpha bonus (the other loses "alone" status). Total damage with 2 attackers ≈ 7 vs 1 attacker solo = 5 — concentrated solo damage is harder to chump-block efficiently. **Never multi-attack with Alpha Strike Traders unless one of the attackers is buffed by Direct Market Access static.**
- **Quant's PCD lord is the linchpin.** Killing PCD twice (HF's only successful disruption) was the only way HF made a dent. Quant should protect PCD.
- **Liquidity Provision is a TRAP at full mana.** Both pilots flagged this — Quant cast LP at full mana twice for 0 net gain (LP gains 3 mana up to current max; if you're already at max, you waste the cast). Only cast LP when current available < max - 2 OR when chaining a 4+ cost play this turn.
- **Trample overflow makes chumping expensive.** Pilot B leaked 2-5 face per chump-block all game. Risk Manager's 4 toughness was barely enough; weaker blockers (Clerk 1/2, FMA 1/3) leaked 2-3 trample per block.
- **Turn 1-5 survival window for Quant**: expect to take 8-12 face damage even with optimal blocking, because no Quant Trader survives an Alpha Strike alone in the 1-2 mana range and Quant can't deploy a 2-toughness wall before T2.

## Engine bug punch list (iter-1, both pilots flagged — for engine team)

These are confirmed by both reports independently. Highest priority first.

1. **Combat damage threshold is wrong.** Both pilots observed "any damage = death" behaviour intermixed with trample-style overflow. The kill rule should be `damage >= toughness`, and overflow to face should ONLY apply when an attacker has the (currently-implicit-by-default) Trample keyword. Either trample is enabled globally and shouldn't be, or the death threshold is broken at `>= 1`. Audit `src/engine/finance_combat.py` damage assignment.
2. **Multi-attacker Alpha asymmetry.** When 2+ Traders attack, the first declared gets +3 alpha (count==1 at trigger time); subsequent declared get nothing. Either fire alpha once at end-of-attacker-declare with the final count, OR document the asymmetry and have AI never multi-attack with Alpha Strikers.
3. **Low-Latency Strike doesn't remove summoning sickness.** Pilot A T11: cast LLS while Bandwidth Predator had SS, BP still couldn't attack. Resolve sets `o.state.summoning_sickness = False` but the change isn't sticking. Likely `event.payload.get("controller")` lookup mismatch — verify in `src/cards/finance/fina/high_frequency.py:_low_latency_strike_resolve`.
4. **Direct Market Access alpha upgrade flag is unwired.** ETB sets `state.turn_data[f"fin_alpha_strike_upgrade_{controller}"]` but `_alpha_strike_bonus` never reads that flag — always uses `power_mod=3`. DMA is currently a 3-mana no-op except for the apparent +4/+0 static buff Pilot B observed (which fired correctly on T17 — note: pilots disagree slightly here, see Contested Questions). Audit which path actually applies the buff.
5. **Speed Amplifier orphans on attached Trader's death.** Stays attached to a dead object_id contributing nothing. Either die-with-target (leaves-battlefield trigger) or auto-re-attach to next highest-power Trader.
6. **Tick Data Archive's "attacked alone last turn" trigger never fires.** The `fin_alpha_struck_alone_<controller>` flag isn't being set by `_alpha_strike_bonus` or `_count_attacking_traders` when count==1. Asset is currently dead.
7. **Correlation Matrix DRAW event doesn't actually draw cards.** Pilot B cast it on T20 expecting +3 cards, got 0. Same bug class probably hits Information Advantage, Sharpe Ratio Alert, Quant Signal — audit all `make_strategy(resolve=...)` and `make_order(resolve=...)` paths that emit DRAW directly.
8. **Quant Signal LOOK_AT_TOP doesn't put a card in hand.** Same bug class — emits LOOK_AT_TOP with `put_to_hand=1` but no card lands in hand.
9. **Risk Manager Arbitrage timing.** Pilot B noted RM's BLOCK_DECLARED interceptor heals BEFORE damage assignment — useless because damage hasn't been applied yet. Should fire AFTER combat damage (e.g. on COMBAT_DAMAGE_DEALT or end-of-combat phase event).
