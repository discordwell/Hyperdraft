# FINM Market Meltdown

`FINM` adds 180 Finance TCG cards across six archetypes:

- Credit Covenant
- Treasury Coupon
- Risk Hedge
- Activist All-In
- Distressed Restructure
- M&A Buyback

The implementation lives in `src/cards/finance/finm/` and is aggregated through
`src/cards/finance/__init__.py`.

See `logs/finance_finm_balance_summary.md` for the nine balance passes and
final starter matrix.
