# HYPERDRAFT — brand & design philosophy

## Brand mark

Always write **HYPERDRAFT** in full caps, everywhere it appears as a brand
mark or in user-visible copy. Code identifiers and the GitHub repo path
(`discordwell/Hyperdraft`) keep their existing casing.

## Retired tagline

"Eight engines, one frame" is **out**. The product will ship far more than
eight engines; the tagline boxed itself in. Do not reintroduce it. Strip
it on sight.

## The pitch

Get back to the feeling of rummaging through the game cabinet during a
sleepover and finding a bunch of games. *None of us have played them. None
of us know the metas.* HYPERDRAFT lets you have that first-time-play
experience on demand — pull a TCG off the shelf, read the rules, figure
it out as you go.

## The audience

People who were told they were smart as children and it stuck. Adults who
treat unfamiliar rules as a puzzle, not friction. They don't want a
tutorial; they want a calm room and an interesting box. Every product
decision — copy, layout, onboarding, defaults — has to read to that
audience without flattering them and without dumbing things down.

## On the laboratory archetype

The lab visual (paper/ink/sodium, Instrument Serif + Geist + Geist Mono,
hairline rules, tabular grids) is the calm room that supports the cabinet
metaphor. Keep it.

**Do NOT lead** with "see the rules running" / "debugger for TCGs" /
"inspect the pipeline" — those are over-indexed; every TCG engine has an
event stack, none of them are revolutionary for having one. The pipeline
is good engineering, not the headline pitch.

## Copy guardrails

When writing user-visible copy:

- ✓ "Pull one off the shelf." / "First-time play, on demand." / "No metas, no tutorial — figure it out."
- ✗ "Eight engines, one frame." / "Debugger for the game of TCGs." / "Watch the rules run."
