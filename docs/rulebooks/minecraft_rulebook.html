I. Welcome to Minecraft TCG

Minecraft TCG is a two-player strategic card game set in the blocky, procedurally generated world you know. Each player controls an Avatar — a lone survivor with 20 hit points — navigating a world of hostile mobs, biome riches, and the creeping dread of Phyrexian corruption. Cards represent the creatures, tools, structures, and actions that define every Minecraft playthrough.

Unlike most card games where life is lost passively, Minecraft TCG revolves around material economy. You mine biomes to gather Wood, Stone, Iron, Redstone, and Diamond — then spend those materials to deploy Mobs on the battlefield, erect Structures on a 3×3 grid behind your Avatar, craft Tools that empower your Avatar's attacks, and unleash one-time Actions. The side that builds the better engine wins.

What makes Minecraft TCG unique in the Hyperdraft collection:

  • The Bed mechanic — your Avatar can respawn indefinitely until your Bed is destroyed first.
  • A physical 3×3 grid — Structures and Blocks occupy real board positions. Column targeting means your grid layout is a tactical decision.
  • The Day/Night cycle — every two turns, the world flips, granting bonuses to builders by day and buffing Hostile mobs at night.
  • Phyrexian corruption — a second win condition: accumulate 5 oil counters through Infect damage and your Avatar is Compleated, which counts as a loss.
At a Glance
Players: 2  |  Deck size: ~50 cards  |  Starting hand: 6 cards  |  Starting HP: 20  |  Game length: 18–36 turns (approx. 20–45 min)

Starter Decks

⛏ Builder Deck

~50 cards. Emphasises Workers, ramp Structures, and mid-range threats. Ramp into material income, defend with Walls and Blocks, then overwhelm through resource conversion into powerful mid-game Mobs.

💎 Miner Deck

~50 cards. Mining bonus Workers, high-value Tools, and Avatar-direct power. Build up mining production, equip powerful weapons, and leverage Beacon synergies for burst Avatar damage.

⚔ Raider Deck

~50 cards. Aggressive Hostile and Raider subtypes, removal actions, iron weapons. Deploy cheap bodies early, chip the Avatar with on-attack triggers, finish with burn actions or Ravager destruction.

🦠 Compleated Dominion

~48 cards. Phyrexian Compleated lords, sweepers, and drain effects. Ramp into Compleated threats; win via Infect oil-counter accumulation or drain combos from Sheoldred and friends.

👁 Box of Horrors

~50 cards. Horror tribal, mind-erosion, cursed structures, and possession mechanics. Establish cheap defensive structures, use discard and mill to weaken the opponent's hand, then convert wounded enemy Mobs into your own Horror army.

World State

The game begins in Day Phase. The active player is determined by the game server (coin flip equivalent). There is no "skip draw on first turn" rule in Minecraft TCG — both players draw 1 card at the start of their first turn.

💡 Mulligan — First Free, Then Pay One

After drawing your opening hand, you may shuffle it back and redraw 6 fresh cards. Your first mulligan is free. Every subsequent mulligan permanently costs one card from your kept hand — placed on the bottom of your library (highest-cost cards first by default).

IV. How to Play — Turn Structure

Each turn flows through five sequential phases. There is no priority system or stack in Minecraft TCG — all card effects resolve immediately when they trigger.

1
Turn Start — Dawn Procedures
Automatic — no player action required
  • Untap all your tapped Mobs (Workers and attackers).
  • Clear summoning sickness from Mobs that survived a full round.
  • Reset all biome mined-this-turn flags — every biome is minable again.
  • Each Structure on your grid that has a turn bonus fires: gain materials or draw cards as listed on the card.
  • Each Compleated Mob you control deals 1 damage to your own Avatar (Phyrexian upkeep tax).
  • If the Day/Night cycle has elapsed (every full round of 2 turns), flip the phase.
2
Draw — Replenish Your Hand
Automatic
Draw 1 card from the top of your deck. Some Structures (Chest, Lectern of Whispers) already granted you additional draws in Phase 1.
3
Main Phase — Lay Your Plans
Your full action window. Actions can be taken in any order, any number of times.

Actions Available This Phase

ActionWho can do itLimitNotes
Mine a Biome Avatar or any untapped Worker Mob 1 per biome per turn (any actor) Each biome can be mined once per turn — by your Avatar or one Worker, not both. Avatar uses its avatar action slot (1/turn). Workers tap after mining.
Explore a Biome Avatar only Uses avatar action (1/turn) Upgrades one biome to the next tier (e.g. Forest → Old Growth Forest).
Play a Card You Unlimited (if you can pay) Pay the material cost; place Mob on creature row, Structure/Block on grid cell, Tool equips to gear slot, Action resolves immediately.
Declare Attackers Your Mobs Once per turn total Tap Mobs you want to attack, assign each a column target. Triggers the Combat Phase.
Avatar Attack Avatar Uses avatar action (1/turn) Requires an equipped Weapon. Deals weapon damage to the frontmost target in a chosen column, or directly to the opponent's Avatar if the column is empty.
☀ Day Phase Bonuses
During Day phase: your first mine action each turn yields +1 of that biome's primary material. The first Structure or Block you play costs 1 less of its cheapest material (Wood or Stone).
🌙 Night Phase Bonus
During Night phase: all your Hostile Mobs gain +1 Attack.
4
Combat Phase — Clash
Triggered when you declare attackers. Skipped if you don't.
  1. Declare Attackers: Tap each attacking Mob and assign it a column (0, 1, or 2).
  2. Declare Blockers: Your opponent assigns untapped Mobs as blockers — one blocker per attacker.
  3. Deal Damage:
    • An attacker without a blocker hits the frontmost object in its column (row 2 → 1 → 0). If the column is empty, it hits the opponent's Avatar.
    • A blocked attacker and its blocker deal damage to each other simultaneously.
    • Unless the attacker has Ranged, it receives return damage equal to the blocker's power.
    • If damage destroys the frontmost block but power remains, overflow damage carries to the next target.
  4. Resolve Deaths: Mobs and Structures at 0 HP go to the graveyard. Deathrattles fire here. Avatar deaths trigger respawn checks.
5
Turn End — Pass the Torch
Automatic
The turn passes to your opponent. The Day/Night cycle counter advances by 1.
Quick Turn Cheat Sheet
1. Start: Untap and reset biomes → start-of-turn bonuses (Structures + Compleated tax in the same pass) → Day/Night flip
2. Draw: Draw 1 card
3. Main: Mine / Explore / Play cards / Attack (any order, any number)
4. Combat: If you attacked — blockers declared, damage resolved, deaths checked
5. End: Pass to opponent

V. Card Types & the Play Area

[ Play area screenshot ]

Your Side of the Table

🧱 The Grid (3×3)

Three columns (0–2) × three rows (front-2, mid-1, back-0). Structures and Blocks occupy one cell each. Combat targets the frontmost occupied cell in a column first.

⚔ Creature Row

All Mobs you control live here. They can attack opponent columns or block incoming Mobs.

👤 Avatar Gear Slots

Three slots: Weapon (enables avatar attacks), Armor (reduces incoming damage), Tool (boosts mining yield). Only one card per slot.

🌲 Biome Lanes

Three biomes per player: Forest, Hills, Cave. Not a zone — stored as game state. Each can be mined once per turn per actor.

🃏 Hand

Cards held but not yet played. No maximum hand-size limit.

🪦 Graveyard

Destroyed cards, used Actions, discarded gear on respawn. Some Actions interact with the graveyard.

Card Types

🐺 Mob (MC_MOB)

Mobs live on the Creature Row. They can attack or be declared as blockers. A new Mob has summoning sickness and cannot attack or mine until the following turn. Mobs have Power (attack) / Toughness (HP). When toughness reaches 0, they go to the graveyard.

Subtype Worker — can mine biomes by tapping. Subtype Hostile — gains +1 Attack at Night. Subtype Compleated — deals 1 damage to its controller each turn start.

🏠 Structure (MC_STRUCTURE)

Structures are placed on a specific cell of your 3×3 grid. They cannot attack or block Mobs directly. Many trigger turn bonuses (materials, card draw). They have Toughness (durability) and are destroyed when it reaches 0.

The frontmost Structure in each column absorbs column-targeted attacks before the Avatar can be hit.

🧱 Block (MC_BLOCK)

A subtype of Structure — smaller, cheaper, purely defensive grid fillers. Blocks with the Wall subtype stop Mobs with the Climb keyword.

⛏ Tool (MC_TOOL)

Tools equip to one of the Avatar's three gear slots. A Tool card is played directly from hand and immediately occupies its slot, replacing any prior card in that slot (the replaced card goes to the graveyard).

  • Weapon slot — grants the Avatar an attack action each turn that deals weapon damage.
  • Armor slot — reduces incoming damage to the Avatar by the stated amount.
  • Tool slot — adds mining yield when the Avatar mines a biome.

All gear is discarded to the graveyard if the Avatar respawns via the Bed.

💥 Action (MC_ACTION)

Actions are one-shot spells. Pay the material cost from your hand, resolve the effect immediately, then the card goes to the graveyard. Actions can be played at any point during your Main Phase.

VI. Resources & Materials

Materials are the currency of Minecraft TCG. There is no "land" or "energy" system — your income depends entirely on how efficiently you mine and build your economy.

Gaining Materials

1. Mining (Primary)
During your Main Phase, tap an untapped Worker Mob or use your Avatar action to mine one of your biomes. Each biome may be mined only once per turn — your Avatar and your Workers compete for the same three biome slots. With three biomes that means up to three mining actions per turn. The action card Bone Meal resets a Worker (untaps and re-enables that biome); Redstone Contraption resets up to two.
2. Day Bonus (Once per day turn)
During Day phase, your first mine action that turn gains +1 of the biome's primary material. Example: Forest normally yields 1 Wood. On a Day turn, the first Forest mine yields 2 Wood.
3. Structure Turn Bonuses (Automatic)
Each turn, every Structure on your grid with a listed turn bonus adds materials to your pool automatically. Key examples: Crafting Table (+1W), Furnace (+1I), Redstone Engine (+1R), Chest (draw 1).

Biome Upgrades

Use your Avatar's explore action (uses avatar action slot) to upgrade a biome. Each biome has three tiers:

VII. Winning & Losing

⚔ Primary Win Condition

Deal enough damage to reduce your opponent's Avatar to 0 or fewer HP while they control no Bed on the grid.

The last player whose Avatar is still alive wins.

The Bed — Respawn Mechanic

If a player's Avatar reaches 0 HP and they control a Bed Structure anywhere on the grid, instead of losing:

  • Their Avatar returns to 20 HP.
  • All equipped gear (Weapon, Armor, Tool) is discarded to the graveyard.
  • The Bed remains — it can trigger unlimited respawns until the Bed itself is destroyed.
  • Multiple Beds don't stack respawn capacity; all Beds must be destroyed before lethal damage sticks.
🦠 Alternate Loss — Phyrexian Compleation
If a player accumulates 5 or more oil counters on their Avatar (placed by Infect-keyword Mobs dealing combat damage), that player loses immediately, regardless of remaining HP. Oil counters are not removed by respawn.
📋 Key Sequencing Rule
To win against a player with a Bed:
  1. First, destroy all of their Beds (Mobs attacking the Bed's column, or Actions like TNT Blast).
  2. Then, reduce the Avatar to 0 HP in the same or a subsequent attack.
This makes Bed placement one of the most important tactical decisions in the game.

VIII. Special Mechanics

📅 Day / Night Cycle

The world flips between Day and Night every full round (once per player per 2-turn cycle). Day favours builders; Night favours aggression.

Day: +1 material on your first mine; first Structure/Block played costs 1 less.
Night: All Hostile Mobs gain +1 Attack until the next flip.

Strategy: Deploy cheap Hostile Mobs just before the Night flip for immediate bonus. Save your first mine for your best biome on Day turns to maximise the day bonus.
⚒ Biome Mining & Upgrading

Your three biomes are your income engine. Each biome may only be mined once per turn — by your Avatar or one Worker, not both. The Avatar's mining uses its single avatar-action slot; Workers consume only their own tap.

Use Explore Map (1W Action) or your Avatar's Explore action to upgrade a biome permanently. The Cave → Diamond Depths upgrade is the most powerful: yields 1S + 1I + 1D per turn.

Example: With 3 Workers you can have each Worker mine a different biome (3 mines total). Adding your Avatar would only matter if you could re-enable a biome — for instance, casting Bone Meal untaps a Worker AND resets every biome's "mined" flag, letting that turn's economy double.
🦠 Infect & Phyrexian Compleation

When a Mob with the Infect keyword deals combat damage to the opponent's Avatar, instead of (or in addition to) dealing HP damage, it deposits oil counters equal to the damage. At 5+ oil counters the player loses immediately.

Certain Actions can also add or remove oil counters. Oil counters do not reset on Avatar respawn — they persist across deaths.

Example cards: Plague Myr (1/1 Infect), Phyrexian Crusader (Aerial + Infect), Skithiryx (Aerial + Infect + Haste).
🐉 Avatar Gear System

Your Avatar has three gear slots. Equipping a new Tool to an occupied slot replaces the old one (old card → graveyard). On Avatar respawn, all three slots are wiped.

  • Weapon: Enables the Avatar Attack action. Damage and keywords (Ranged, Infect, Aerial) come from the equipped weapon.
  • Armor: Subtracts a flat amount from all incoming damage to the Avatar (e.g., Iron Armor reduces each hit by 2).
  • Tool: Adds to mining yield each time the Avatar mines (e.g., Iron Pickaxe grants +1 Iron per mine).
🐺 Worker Mobs

Mobs with the Worker subtype can tap to mine one biome per turn. They can still block or attack, but tapping to mine exhausts them — a tapped Worker cannot block.

Many Workers have a mining bonus that adds extra materials when they mine. Examples: Steve's Helper (+1W per mine), Villager Mason (+1S), Allay Courier (+1R).

Workers die easily — protect them behind Walls. Losing your Workers collapses your economy.

Combat Keywords

Haste

Ignores summoning sickness. Can attack or mine the same turn it enters play.

Example: Alex's Scout, Skithiryx.
Aerial

Bypasses Blocks entirely — flies over them to hit the next target or Avatar directly. Can be blocked only by Mobs with Reach or Aerial.

Example: Ghast, Blaze Wraith.
Reach

Can block Aerial attackers. Has no offensive benefit.

Example: Snow Golem, Skeleton Archer.
Climb

Ignores Wall-subtype Blocks and Structures when determining column targets. Cannot be stopped by a Wall — but can be stopped by non-Wall Structures.

Example: Spider, Cave Crawler.
Ranged

When this Mob attacks and is blocked, it takes no return damage from the blocker. The blocker still takes normal damage.

Example: Skeleton Archer, Blaze.
Siege

After this Mob deals damage to a Block (MC_BLOCK subtype), the Block is also destroyed, regardless of remaining HP.

Example: Ravager (also gains a +1/+1 counter when a Block dies).
💀 Deathrattle

A triggered ability that fires when the card is destroyed and moves to the graveyard. Deathrattles resolve before replacement effects from new tokens can claim priority.

Example: Creeper (deals 3 to the frontmost in its attacked column on death).
🩸 Drain

An effect that simultaneously deals damage to the opponent's Avatar and heals the same amount to yours.

Example: Sheoldred, the Whispering One (drain 5).

X. Quick Reference

▌ TURN ORDER
1 Turn Start Untap + reset biomes → start-of-turn bonuses (Structures & Compleated tax) → Day/Night flip
2 Draw Draw 1 card
3 Main Phase Mine / Explore / Play cards / Declare attacks / Avatar attack — any order, unlimited
4 Combat Blockers declared → damage dealt → deaths resolved (only if you attacked)
5 End Turn Pass to opponent. Day/Night counter +1.
☀ DAY / 🌙 NIGHT
Day: +1 on first mine; first Structure/Block costs 1 less
Night: Hostile Mobs gain +1 ATK
Flip: Every full round (every 2 turns in a 2-player game)
💀 WIN / LOSE
Win: Opponent Avatar at 0 HP with no Bed
Respawn: Avatar at 0 HP + Bed present → back to 20 HP, lose all gear
Compleation: 5+ oil counters → instant loss
🔑 KEY INTERACTIONS
Q: Can my Avatar mine AND have a Worker mine the same biome?
A: No. Each biome can be mined once per turn, by either your Avatar or one Worker. The "mined" flag is shared across actors. Bone Meal and Redstone Contraption reset biome mined flags as a side effect of untapping Workers, which is the only way to mine the same biome twice in one turn.

Q: Does Aerial bypass all Blocks?
A: Yes — Aerial attackers skip over MC_BLOCK type cards. They can still be stopped by Mobs with Reach or Aerial, or by Structures (non-Block type) in the column.

Q: Do oil counters reset on respawn?
A: No. Oil counters persist through Avatar deaths. Bed respawn resets HP only.

Q: If I equip a new Weapon over my current one, what happens?
A: The old Weapon goes to your graveyard immediately. The new Weapon equips.

Q: Can Workers attack?
A: Yes, but they tap when they mine. A tapped Worker cannot attack that turn (and vice versa — if a Worker attacks and taps, it cannot mine that turn).
